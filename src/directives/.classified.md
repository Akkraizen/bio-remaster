Clearance ranks by secrets found: SCRIPT_KIDDIE (0), OPERATOR (4), NETRUNNER (8), GHOST (12), ROOT (18).
Shown in CONFIG → Secrets and in `whoami`.

From 8 secrets the terminal `hint` command gives a riddle about a random missing achievement.

At 18/18: a finale scene with a thank-you message, the ROOT theme, a ROOT ACCESS badge in the footer,
a "Replay finale" button in CONFIG → Secrets and the `finale` terminal command.

CONFIG → Secrets can export a progress card (PNG): Share (system share sheet on phones) or Download.

Bonus, no achievement:

- open the browser console for a greeting;
